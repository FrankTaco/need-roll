# Need Roll

Roll 1 to 100 to settle anything: who gets the last slice, who does the dishes, which restaurant tonight. Solo rolls, versus the house, and pass-and-play on one machine, with stats, streaks, achievements, daily and weekly quests, and unlockable themes, avatars, roll animations, and sound packs.

## Download

Grab `NeedRoll-<version>.exe` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest). No installer: put it anywhere and double-click.

- Needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
- The first time you run it Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" because the build is not yet code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- Your stats and unlocks are saved under your Windows profile, so moving or re-downloading the exe keeps your progress.

This repository holds only the downloadable builds.
